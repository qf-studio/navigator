# Pitfall: Pilot's test gate shows the last 20 lines of stderr only, with a 5-minute cap

## Summary
The quality gate on the Pilot box captures stderr only and shows its last 20 lines; the test gate times out at 5 minutes. Stdout headers and summaries are invisible, and a slow suite times out.

## Context
2026-11-06: GH-40 and GH-41 each failed three gate passes blind; the tail showed only OK lines and 'make: *** Error 1'. GH-41 then timed out at exactly 5m0s.

## Details
Makefile 'test' now runs with exec 1>&2, runs suites in parallel (TEST_JOBS), echoes output in TEST_DIRS order and ends with a FAILED SUITES block naming each red suite and its FAIL/ERROR lines.

## Recommended Approach
Keep anything a gate must see on stderr and in the last 20 lines. Keep make test under ~2 minutes on a 4-vCPU host.
